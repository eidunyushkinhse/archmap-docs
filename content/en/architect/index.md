Architects create projects and are responsible for what's in them: the system structure, relationships, logic diagrams, and facts from the code. To do this, you need the Architect account role (an administrator grants it) and the owner or editor role in the project.

This guide covers working on the canvas and the ways to fill a project: manually, from YAML files and archives, from a repository with an AI agent, and via MCP. Reading diagrams is covered in the [User guide](../user/index.md) and isn't repeated here.
